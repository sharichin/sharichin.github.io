Engineering Entrepreneurship Speaker Series

From Ideation to Product and Business Development: Growing a Successful Business Supporting Technical Fields in the Information Age

Pilot · 2026
August 2026 — Black Business Month
Portland, OR + Virtual
3 sessions across 6 weeks

NSBE Portland Professionals is bringing Black and Brown engineers together as a community — alongside entrepreneurs and community stakeholders — for a three-session series exploring AI & climate impact, green energy entrepreneurship, and early-stage funding. Funded by the Lemelson Foundation, the series is designed to create tangible pathways — not just programming.
